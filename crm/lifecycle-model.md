# Customer Lifecycle Operating Model

> Fictional SaaS operating model for portfolio demonstration.

## Principle

Lifecycle stages should describe a meaningful customer state and create a corresponding operational response. A stage is useful when it helps the team decide **what should happen next**.

| Stage | Meaning | Primary Objective | Example Motion |
|---|---|---|---|
| Lead | Evaluating NexaFlow | Reach meaningful product evaluation | Trial guidance and qualification |
| Activated | Account has started but has not fully established repeat value | Reach first/second key value milestone | Structured onboarding |
| Active | Consistent product value and engagement | Maintain value and identify growth | Success cadence / expansion |
| At Risk | Signals indicate meaningful loss of engagement or satisfaction | Understand and recover value | Risk intervention |
| Churned | Paid relationship has ended | Learn why and preserve future opportunity | Churn feedback / eligible win-back |
| Reactivated | Former customer has returned | Re-establish durable value | Reactivation success plan |

## Stage Movement

The model is deliberately non-linear. An Active customer can move to At Risk when meaningful behavioral signals deteriorate. An At Risk customer can recover to Active. A Churned customer can become Reactivated.

## Health vs. Lifecycle

Health score and lifecycle stage are related but not interchangeable. Lifecycle describes the account's operating state; health helps prioritize attention within and across states.

The thresholds used in this repository are illustrative synthetic rules, not universal CRM benchmarks.
