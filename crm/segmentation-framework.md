# CRM Segmentation Framework

## Objective

Translate customer attributes and behavioral signals into differentiated CRM actions rather than sending the same journey to every account.

## Example Dynamic Segments

### High-Value Healthy
**Illustrative rule:** health ≥ 80 and MRR ≥ $1,000.

**Motion:** expansion discovery, advocacy and relationship strengthening.

### High-Value Risk
**Illustrative rule:** health < 55 and MRR ≥ $1,000.

**Motion:** prioritize human review and personalized retention intervention rather than relying only on automation.

### Activation Opportunity
**Rule:** lifecycle stage = Activated.

**Motion:** identify the missing value milestone and help the account complete a key workflow.

### Low Engagement
**Illustrative rule:** paid account with usage below 40%.

**Motion:** contextual education, use-case recommendations and targeted outreach.

### Promoters
**Illustrative rule:** NPS ≥ 9, ideally combined with strong health and sufficient relationship history.

**Motion:** advocacy or case-study opportunity when context supports it.

### Win-Back Eligible
**Rule:** churned account whose churn reason and account context make reactivation appropriate.

**Motion:** timed reactivation journey tied to relevant product/value changes.

## Guardrail

Segmentation should guide prioritization, not erase customer context. High-impact accounts, sensitive situations and ambiguous risk signals may require human review before automated action.
