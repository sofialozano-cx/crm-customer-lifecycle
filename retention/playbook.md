# Retention Operations Playbook

## Operating Principle

A risk signal should lead to a proportionate action. The purpose of lifecycle analytics is not to label customers as red, yellow or green; it is to help the team intervene with better context and timing.

## 1. Detect

Potential signals can include declining usage, increased inactivity, negative sentiment, unresolved product friction, incomplete adoption or a meaningful change from the customer's own baseline.

## 2. Validate

Before acting, review account context. A usage decline may represent risk, seasonality, a completed project or a change in customer workflow. Avoid treating one metric as definitive churn intent.

## 3. Prioritize

Consider severity, account value, lifecycle stage, customer history and whether the intervention should be automated or human-led.

## 4. Intervene

Possible motions:
- targeted education when value is unclear;
- onboarding support when adoption is incomplete;
- personalized CSM outreach for high-value risk;
- product/support coordination when friction is unresolved;
- reactivation journey after eligible churn.

## 5. Learn

Capture the reason for churn, recovery outcome and customer feedback. Repeated patterns should influence onboarding, documentation, product feedback and future CRM segmentation.

## Measurement

Useful operating measures can include risk-to-recovery rate, churn rate by segment, time from risk signal to intervention, activation completion and reactivation rate. This portfolio does not claim real production outcomes for these measures.
